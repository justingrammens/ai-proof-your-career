# AI-Proof Your Career

Slides from my talk at Augsburg University's 2026-27 Innovator Speaker Series, Minneapolis, November 16, 2026.

The talk covers what business students need to know about building a career in the age of AI. It draws on two roles: hiring software engineers at [Lab651](https://lab651.com), and teaching graduate AI and machine learning at the University of St. Thomas.
